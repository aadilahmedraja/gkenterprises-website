Network Architecture

How the product line fits together in a live deployment

A simplified view of a typical FTTH / metro rollout using GK Enterprises equipment at each stage.

01

WDM multiplies capacity onto the fiber

Before the signal reaches the central office equipment, WDM combines multiple wavelengths onto a single strand — scaling capacity without new cable runs.

02

The OLT aggregates and distributes traffic

The OLT sits in the central office, aggregating subscriber traffic from the core network and pushing it out across the PON.

03

EDFA keeps the signal strong over distance

On long-haul or metro spans, the EDFA amplifies the optical signal directly — no electrical conversion, minimal added latency.

04

Fiber optic cable carries it the rest of the way

Single-mode cable handles the trunk and distribution runs; the network narrows down toward the customer premises.

05

The router delivers it to the end user

Single-band or dual-band routers terminate the connection at the customer site and distribute it over Wi-Fi and Ethernet.
